# Brag plan: Maison Regulus

## Rubric
- **What it is:** the one-page FR/EN site of Maison Regulus, a studio that builds custom portfolios and storefront sites ("sites vitrines").
- **Who it's for:** students and professionals (portfolios), businesses and shops (sites vitrines). It gives them a site "sur mesure et à votre image".
- **What sets it apart:** the whole brand is one star. Regulus, "petit roi", α Leonis, the brightest star in Leo, sits under the roof of the house emblem. The site's hero is that constellation.
- **Best claim:** "Beyond what's possible." and "Votre projet sera la prochaine étoile."
- **Visual hook:** the Leo constellation draws itself, Regulus flares, and the star drops under the roof of the emblem as it draws.
- **Real UI to show:** the hero, the two service cards, the niriantsoa.me case study (desktop + mobile), and the contact letter being filled in through to "Message envoyé."
- **Tone:** `polished` with a cinematic opening. Slow, elegant, gold on midnight.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
Every client site is a new star in the house's constellation. The video starts in the night sky and ends by inviting the viewer to be the next star.

## Visual identity (from styles.css / brand kit)
Royal Midnight #0B0F19, Matte Gold #C5A880, Soft Ivory #F4F4F0, highlight #E9D7B4 / #FFF4DD. Cormorant Garamond (display, italic for the tagline) + Inter (small caps labels, wide tracking). Real assets: assets/sky.svg, emblem geometry, logo-fr.svg / logo-en.svg, realisations/*.webp. The real site runs in an iframe for the hero, services and contact scenes.
